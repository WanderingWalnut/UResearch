# Launch with university-bound professor discovery

At launch, a Student can discover and contact only Professors from their own University, starting with University of Calgary profiles. We chose this hard launch boundary because students are most likely to secure research through their own university, and the first reliable dataset will be UCalgary Professor Profiles.

## Consequences

- The launch experience is simpler and easier to seed with high-quality data.
- University email domain validation is part of the product model, not just authentication.
- The data model should still allow future universities and cross-university outreach without rewriting core campaign concepts.
